figure 3 · the attestation

37 bytes that tie a burn to the revenue that paid for it.

after every buyback-and-burn the treasury sends a zero-value transaction to itself. its calldata is a fixed prefix, a version byte and a sha256 of the revenue-event ids consumed.

scheme v1. any other value is not an attestation.
32 bytes

revenue hash
the revenue-event ids the buyback consumed, sorted, comma-joined, hashed. order-independent; any change to the set changes the hash.
