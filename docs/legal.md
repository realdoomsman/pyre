# Legal posture

Internal notes on how the product is structured and why. This is not legal advice; have counsel review before launch and after any change to the money flows.

## Buybacks are not distributions

- 25% of a Solana coin's creator fees funds on-chain purchases of that coin that are immediately burned (`burnChecked` on the Token-2022 mint, so `getTokenSupply` falls), funded only by that coin's own fee share — a Solana app coin's fees never buy $PYRE on either chain. On the 7 legacy Robinhood Chain coins (launched on PONS v2 before launches there closed) the 25% share funds the PYRE refund pool while any snapshot holder is still owed, and once every holder is settled buys $PYRE and burns it (`token.burn`, so `totalSupply` falls); $PYRE already accrued for burning keeps being burned. Robinhood app coins are never bought back by the platform. No holder ever receives tokens, ETH or SOL from the platform because they hold a coin. There is no dividend, yield, claim, or redemption right. The PYRE refund program below is the one payment to snapshot holders, and it repays ETH put in; holding is a condition of it (selling shrinks it), never something it pays for.
- The platform executes every buyback from its own treasury wallets as a product feature; the amount, timing, and existence of buybacks are at the platform's discretion and can be paused (`PlatformSetting`, admin settings endpoint). Each buyback is attested on-chain — a zero-value treasury self-transaction carrying `0x5059524501 || sha256(fee-share ledger entry ids)` on Robinhood Chain, an SPL Memo transaction from the treasury Solana wallet reading `pyre:burn:v1:<sha256>` on Solana — so the record is verifiable without trusting the site.
- Public copy must not describe buybacks as returns, income, rewards, or "value accrual to holders". Approved language: "fees pay an agent to build the app; 25% of every coin's fees buys and burns the coin". For a legacy Robinhood Chain coin: "25% of its fees funds PYRE refunds until every snapshot holder is settled, then buys and burns PYRE". The growth worker's system prompt forbids price talk and the reviewer rejects it inside apps.
- **New coins launch on pump.fun only.** PONS v2 launches are closed permanently; the 7 legacy Robinhood Chain coins keep running (sweeps, builds, trading, holders, launcher payouts unchanged). Public copy says "coins launch on pump.fun" and calls the old ones "legacy Robinhood Chain coins"; it never offers a choice of venue.
- **$PYRE is moving to Solana.** It relaunches as a new coin through a fair launch on pump.fun with no dev buy. The chosen path: the founder creates it from his own wallet and, right after, sets pump.fun creator fee sharing to the treasury Solana wallet as the only shareholder at 100% — a split pump.fun makes final — so the treasury collects all of its creator fees and the founder none (the fallback, creating it from the treasury Solana wallet `pumpAdapter.treasury()` directly, has the same effect). Until that coin launches, only the mint published on pyre.fun (`PYRE_SOL_MINT`) is ours, and every public surface that describes the move (README, terms, content policy, `/pyre` page, `/refund`, posts) says so; the site footer only announces the move and links `/refund`. Any other "PYRE" on Solana or elsewhere is not us. Robinhood Chain $PYRE (`0xc8488bE2e4f430420A364E64f4D8af428b74D903`) is not bridged, wrapped or migrated on-chain: its holders at the snapshot who still hold it are refunded instead.
- **PYRE refunds return ETH put in; they are not a yield or a return on holding.** Each address holding $PYRE at Robinhood Chain block 79819827 (2026-10-04T09:12:28Z, before the move was first announced at 09:18:42Z) is owed `max(0, ETH spent buying − ETH received selling)` over launch → snapshot, capped at what it paid for the $PYRE it still held at the snapshot (average buy cost × min(snapshot balance, PYRE bought); PYRE that only arrived by transfer earns nothing); nothing after the block counts and holding afterwards on either chain earns nothing. **Only addresses that still hold the $PYRE they had at the snapshot are refunded**: the public rule is "you must still hold the PYRE you had at the snapshot. selling or moving it after the snapshot shrinks your refund for good; buying more doesn't raise it." Moving it to another wallet the holder owns counts as moving. The refund is scaled by the lowest balance the address has held since the snapshot (`refundEligibleWei`), so holding can only keep or reduce it, never increase it; credit already allocated before a sale stays payable and is not clawed back. Funding has two sources, both credited to the `REFUND` ledger account only while anyone is still owed: 25% (`REFUND_FEE_BPS`) of the Solana $PYRE coin's creator fees at each claim, and the 25% fee share of the legacy Robinhood Chain coins, which reverts to buying and burning $PYRE once everyone is settled. The pool is allocated pro-rata to remaining eligible refund (denominated in ETH, converted at the ETH price at allocation) and paid in SOL to a Solana wallet the holder proves with signatures from both wallets. Each holder is capped at what they put in and the program stops when everyone is repaid; there is no upside, no interest and no time value. Payouts obey `PAYOUTS_FROZEN` and `pause_refunds`. Copy must say "refund" and "paid back", never "rewards", "yield", "returns", "airdrop" or "income". **Counsel should review** this program — eligibility, the hold-to-stay-eligible condition (whether conditioning a payment on continued holding changes its character), the cap, whether the promise creates an enforceable obligation, redirecting the legacy coins' 25% share from a burn to refunds (what their buyers and launchers were told, and how the change is announced), tax reporting of SOL payouts, and sanctions screening of payout wallets — before payouts start.
- Launcher payments (15% of creator fees) and bounty payouts (ETH escrowed in the treasury, released on a merged PR) are compensation for work: launching and specifying an app, and merged pull requests. Launcher payments are paid in ETH on Robinhood Chain whatever chain the coin is on. There is no contributor carve-out of the fee stream. $PYRE staker slices are paid for providing scheduler priority to an app, not for holding.
- PONS's own creator-tax and holder-fee-sharing features are switched off for every app coin Pyre launched on PONS (`creatorTaxBps = 0`, `buybackEnabled = false`), so the only fee that flows is the venue's base 1%, 70% of which lands in the app wallet. $PYRE itself is the exception: the Robinhood Chain $PYRE was launched from the treasury with a 2.5% creator tax (`creatorTaxBps = 250` on-chain, `buybackEnabled = false`), which goes to the treasury as the coin's creator alongside its 70% share of the 1% and is booked as platform fees; it is never paid to holders. On pump.fun the creator fee is pump.fun's: 30 bps of curve trades and a market-cap-tiered share of PumpSwap trades, set by pump.fun's fee program, changed by pump.fun before and changeable again, with no warranty under pump.fun's terms and subject to its community-takeover (CTO) process, which can re-route a coin's creator fees away from the app wallet. Pyre never launches with pump.fun's holder-reward, cashback or tokenized-agent options, so no pump.fun mechanism pays holders either. Copy and terms must not promise fee revenue on either venue.

## Custodial wallets and trading

- Custodial wallets are HD keys derived on the server from `PLATFORM_MASTER_SEED_HEX`: `m/44'/60'/0'/0/<index>` (secp256k1) on Robinhood Chain and `m/44'/501'/<index>'/0'` (SLIP-0010 ed25519) on Solana. The browser never signs; every on-chain action for a custodial user is a server-signed transaction triggered by an authenticated request. External-wallet users sign their own transactions in the browser on Robinhood Chain. **Solana is custodial-only**: there is no Solana wallet sign-in and no browser-wallet trading on Solana; a user who wants to trade a Solana coin with their own key is sent to pump.fun. The one browser Solana signature anywhere on Pyre is the PYRE refund link at `/refund`: an ed25519 signature over a fixed text message that proves the payout wallet. It signs no transaction and moves nothing.
- The site executes buys and sells only against the same public venue everyone uses — PONS curve or Uniswap v4 pool on Robinhood Chain, pump.fun curve or PumpSwap pool on Solana — at the user's request, with the user's slippage. Pyre takes no spread and no fee on trades. Copy says "trade", never "invest"; quotes are labelled estimates.
- Withdrawals are capped per user per day (`WITHDRAW_DAILY_CAP_USD`) as a blast-radius limit for a stolen session, and the cap is published in the terms and on the account page.
- Deleting an account retires the derived key; the account page tells users to withdraw first.

## Apps take no money

- Hosted apps are free to use. There is no checkout, subscription, per-request charge, purchase, or ad slot in any Pyre app, and `@pyre/app-sdk` exposes no way to ask a user for money. The platform is therefore not a merchant of record for anything, issues no receipts, and has nothing to refund.
- The only coin-linked feature inside an app is a holder tier: a feature that opens when the signed-in wallet holds at least N of the app's coin, decided by a `balanceOf` read. Holding is never charged and never pays out; it is a product feature, not a purchase or a return.
- The money a user can move on Pyre is limited to buying and selling coins on the public venue, staking the refundable launch stake (1 SOL; the legacy Robinhood Chain coins were staked with 0.05 ETH), topping up an app's build budget in ETH, and depositing to or withdrawing from a custodial wallet on either chain. Every one of those is described in `docs/economics.md` and the terms. Snapshot $PYRE holders can additionally link a Solana wallet to receive PYRE refunds; linking moves no money.

## Code licensing

- Every generated repository is created public under the MIT license (`GITHUB_OWNER/pyre-<slug>`).
- No IP assignment: launchers keep whatever rights they have in their prompt and spec; the platform keeps rights in the template, SDK, and platform code; generated output is released under MIT to everyone. Contributors submit PRs under the repository's MIT license.
- The name, ticker, and image supplied at launch are the launcher's responsibility and are written immutably to the chain by the pump.fun `create_v2` (for a legacy coin, by the PONS launch). The metadata JSON that pump.fun reads is served by Pyre at `/v1/apps/:slug/metadata.json`; the on-chain name, symbol and URI are fixed at creation. The impersonation category exists in the classifier and the report form; takedowns remove the coin from Pyre's pages and stop the app, they cannot alter the chain.

## Moderation and takedowns

- Intake: every prompt passes a classifier (`ModerationVerdict`: SCAM, PHISHING, GAMBLING, ILLEGAL, IMPERSONATION, ADULT, HATE, OTHER). Rejected prompts never become apps.
- Deploy: a reviewer model blocks diffs that add auth/wallet code, anything that asks users for money, external scripts, raw network access, exfiltration, or violate the content policy.
- Reports: `POST /v1/reports` (ABUSE, DMCA, IMPERSONATION, OTHER). Admins action reports and can kill an app (`KILLED` → 410, coin untouched).
- DMCA: designate an agent, publish the contact in `docs/privacy.md`/terms, follow the flow in `docs/runbook.md`. Counter-notices restore within the statutory window.

## Not legal advice

Nothing in the docs, the site, or the feed is legal, tax, or investment advice. Coins launched through pump.fun on Solana, and the legacy coins launched through PONS v2 on Robinhood Chain, are third-party assets traded on third-party contracts and programs (pump.fun, PumpSwap, PONS, Uniswap v4) — the PONS contracts have not completed a public audit, and pump.fun's programs and fee rules are pump.fun's to change; the platform custodies user funds only in the custodial wallets and treasury operations described in `docs/economics.md`. Users interact with Robinhood Chain and Solana at their own risk.
